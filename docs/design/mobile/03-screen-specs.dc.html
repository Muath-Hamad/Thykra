Thykra mobile · Compose MultiplatformPart 3 of 4Deliverable 4 — screen specs, every route

Twelve routes,
one thumb.

Artboards are 393 × 852 dp, Paper on the left, Darkroom beside it, then the states that change the layout. Rows scroll sideways. Each screen carries the same six-part spec: regions, DTO mapping, states, interactions, TalkBack order, RTL. Nothing on a board is a new component — every part is from Part 2, every colour from Part 1.

Ordered by journey depth, not by route table. Invite (J2), Trip (J3) and the dock (J6) come first and go deepest; the rest are shorter because they are lists of kit parts. The three densest screens are re-checked at 360 × 780 at the end, and the viewer is the only screen drawn in landscape.

Every screen: Scaffold with safeDrawing insets, top bar 56 dp with tonalElevation 0, content in a single LazyColumn (or staggered grid), pull-to-refresh on every root list, predictive back on nested routes, skeletons after 200 ms, offline banner under the top bar when disconnected. Those defaults are stated once here and not repeated per screen.

Picker → dock → chapters

Photo Picker returns URIs; the queue reads EXIF DateTimeOriginal locally (Android ExifInterface, iOS CGImageSource, via expect/actual) before uploading, so the placeholder plate can join the right chapter immediately; the server's takenAt replaces it on confirm. Files over the server limit fail locally as "Too large (2.1 GB)" with Skip instead of Retry flag: limit must be known client-side; add to /api/config or hardcode. Batch progress = confirmed bytes ÷ total bytes; the header count is DONE ÷ total.

Backgrounding · reopen

WorkManager continues; a foreground-service notification (Android only, in androidMain) mirrors the dock header: "Uploading 17 of 43 to Wadi Rum" with a determinate bar; tap → deep link to the trip. On reopen the dock re-mounts from the persisted queue in whatever state it is: still uploading, complete (celebration fires now if it never showed), or failed (Retry all). iOS gets the same dock; background continuation there is best-effort with BGProcessingTask and the dock says "Keep Thykra open to finish" when it cannot guarantee it.

Share-to-Thykra target

Registered as a share target for image/* and video/*. Opens a ThykraSheet over a blank shell: thumbnail strip of what was shared (≤ 3 + "+N"), title "Add N photos to…", trips ordered by last upload with a "Recent" badge on the first, New trip row → Create-trip sheet (§11) then enqueue. Selecting a trip enqueues and navigates into it with the dock already showing; the sheet never asks twice. Signed out → Landing with the intent held, then the picker.

Celebration · TalkBack

Dock turns successContainer, Check disc scales in with spring, one Confirm haptic, copy in numbers; new plates in the grid get a 2 dp primary outline that fades over dur4 after See them. Fires once per trip per batch (batch id stored). TalkBack hears the header once as a polite live region: "43 photos and 3 videos are in. Sorted into 2 days." Reduced motion: no scale, cross-fade only.

What changes at 360

Display steps down one slot. The TripActionBar drops the Share label and becomes icon-only 44 dp (the label returns from 380 dp) — Add photos keeps its label because it is the primary. Masthead cover is still 16:9 (203 dp). Invite cover shrinks to 250 dp (32% of 780). Failed dock rows stack filename over status instead of side by side; percentages stay inline. The ReactionPicker wraps to 2 × 4. Nothing else moves.

At 200% type

Body and labels double; display caps at 1.5×. Verified layouts: chapter header (numeral 60 sp, date wraps under it rather than beside — FlowRow), pinned bar grows to 56 dp, action bar buttons grow to 56 dp and the bar to 72 + inset, dock rows to 64 dp, nav labels wrap to two lines (Material allows it). Cards never fix a height. The invite button column scrolls with the content instead of anchoring when the content is taller than the window.

Tablet (≥ 600 dp) in one line

Trips two-up; trip grid 3 columns and the masthead becomes a 2-column row (cover start, title end) — the one place the web's side-by-side masthead returns; nav bar becomes NavigationRail at the start; sheets cap at 560 dp wide and centre; the viewer's filmstrip shows 15 thumbs. Not drawn — every rule above is a parameter, not a new layout.
